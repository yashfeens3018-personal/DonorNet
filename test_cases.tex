\section*{How to Use This Document}
Each test case lists its ID, scenario, steps, test data, expected result and pre-requisite. Fill in the \textbf{Actual Result} and \textbf{Pass/Fail} rows while testing. Passwords are case-sensitive.

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}ll@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{ID Prefix} & \textbf{Area} \\
\midrule
P\_SIGNUP, D\_SIGNUP & Patient / Donor sign-up \\
LOGIN & Login (patient, donor, blood bank) \\
FP & Forgot password \\
SD, SBB & Search donors / blood banks \\
BB\_PREQ & Blood bank: patient requests \\
BB\_STOCK, BB\_MKREQ & Blood bank: stock and blood requests \\
ADMIN & Admin \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
